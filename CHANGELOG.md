# Changelog

## 0.1.47

- **Pick the language you speak in calls.** Whisper guessed the language, and short Slovenian phrases came out as something like Russian. **Settings → Chat → Language in calls** sets it (Slovenian, English, Croatian, German, … or Detect), and a **Language** picker in the call window changes it for that call. Whisper and ElevenLabs are told the language instead of guessing, Windows' recognizers use it where Windows has it, and the agents are asked to answer in it.
- **Agents answer in Slovenian, in the voice you pick.** ElevenLabs' fast Flash model doesn't speak Slovenian, so answers in a call whose language Flash doesn't have are read with Eleven v3. **Settings → Chat** can pick the model instead. Right-click an agent's tile in a call to pick its voice, which it keeps in every call on that computer.
- **Calls through your other computer name you right.** Calling a connected ProJict's agents, the ringing line used your own computer's name ("ssitmi is calling you … answer in @ssitmi"). The agent then answered in a new, empty conversation. Now the lines use the name that ProJict knows you by, and picking up says "@you" for a private conversation instead of its inner name ("#dm-…").
- **Interrupt an agent while it speaks.** A **Stop** button in the call window, or the **Esc** key, cuts the answer off at once, and the answers waiting after it aren't read (they're in the conversation). Speaking over an answer stops it too, when your voice at the microphone is clearly louder than your speakers' sound, and what you say goes through. With a headset that works every time; with loud speakers, Stop is the sure way. ProJict measures the speakers' sound during the first moment of each answer, so speaking over works after that moment; Stop and Esc work from the start.
- **Calls miss less of what you say.** Listening paused for the whole time an answer was read out, including the moment ElevenLabs took to fetch it, before anything could be heard. A short "da", "yes" or "no" was too short to count, and quiet speech could stay under the threshold. Now you're heard until the answer actually plays. Only what starts while it plays is dropped as the speakers' own sound, and it isn't even sent to be transcribed. Something you started saying first still comes through, quick replies count, and quieter speech is heard.
- **Calls with Whisper say when it's getting ready.** Whisper's first load on a computer can take a while (a big model, and the graphics card preparing itself the first time), and calls made meanwhile seemed not to hear you. Now the call window says Whisper is getting ready until it is, and what you say meanwhile waits for it. A line says when it's ready. If it can't get ready, the call says why, and the old recognizer hears you.

## 0.1.46 (2026-11-05)

- **Your phone hears what waits for you at once.** A "You, on your phone" token can keep `GET /api/v1/me/watch` open. It gets a line for each new message to you, the counts of what waits whenever they change (questions, ship plans, work to review, unread), and a ping every 30 quiet seconds.
- **ProJict on your phone, first step: a token that acts as you.** In **Settings › API**, a token's **Tools** can now be **You, on your phone**. It signs as you, not as an agent, and does what only you can: answer what agents ask, approve or decline ship plans, and accept finished work or send it back with why. It also chats, comments and adds items as you, at `/api/v1/me`. Made with network access on, it shows a QR code for the phone to scan, with this computer's addresses (Tailscale's first), the token and the certificate's fingerprint. The Android app that scans it comes next.
- **No stray arrows in Chat's list.** 0.1.45's own heading for a connected ProJict's private messages also drew an empty heading, just an arrow, under your own Channels, Private messages and Archive. It's only there now when there is something to fold.
- **Pausing a project puts its items on hold.** Set a project to **Paused** and all its open items go on hold, so agents leave them alone. Each item remembers its priority. Set the project **Active**, **Released** or **Idea** again and each item gets its priority back. Items that were on hold before stay on hold, a priority you change while the project is paused stays as you set it, and one **Undo** takes it all back.
- **Calls understand what you say much better.** Calls used Windows' old offline recognizer, which often misheard ("talking to Wren" came out as "talking so Lauren"). **Settings → Chat → Understanding you** now offers three better ones, chosen on each computer: **Windows' online dictation** (your voice goes to Microsoft; it needs Online speech recognition on in Windows' privacy settings), **ElevenLabs' speech to text** (with your ElevenLabs key; each phrase costs credits), or **Whisper on this computer** (offline, on the graphics card when it can). For Whisper, download a model once, right there: Base (142 MB), Small (466 MB) or Large v3 Turbo (1.6 GB, the best). Whisper knows your agents' names, so it writes "Wren", not "Ren". Its model loads while a call rings and then stays loaded. When the one you picked can't (no key, no model, dictation off, out of credits), the call says why and the old recognizer hears you meanwhile.
- **Pick the microphone for calls, and see that it hears you.** Calls listened only to Windows' default microphone, and when that wasn't the one you talk into (a VR headset's, say), nothing you said came through, and nothing said why. Now **Settings → Chat → Microphone for calls** picks it, on each computer. A bar under your name in the call window moves when ProJict hears sound. If it hears nothing for a while, the window says which microphone it's listening to and where to change it, including Windows' microphone privacy setting.

## 0.1.45 (2026-11-04)

- **Fold a connected ProJict's private messages on their own.** In Chat, another ProJict's private conversations were listed under its channels in one section. They now have a **Private messages** heading of their own there, which you fold and unfold separately (folding that ProJict still folds everything in it). It's kept on this computer, like the other headings.
- **What you type in Add item… isn't lost any more.** When another ProJict changed a shared board (or you were connected to one and its agents worked there), the board was rebuilt and a category's **Add item…** box came back empty, with the keyboard gone from it. Now it keeps what you typed, and the keyboard goes back to it where you were. Items you're editing and comments you're writing were already kept.
- **Color schemes.** Beside the light and dark theme, **Settings › Board & prompts › Colors** has premade schemes: **Ocean**, **Forest**, **Sunset**, **Rose**, **Grape**, **Gold** and **Slate**. Each changes the accent (buttons, links, selections, check boxes, toggles) and tints the windows' background and title bars, in light and dark. **Custom** lets you pick your own accent and background, and the colors of notice bars, warnings, errors, success and agents at work; it starts from the scheme you had. Colors show as you pick them, and Cancel puts yours back. **Windows**, the default, keeps your Windows accent as before.
- **Disconnect agents.** Right-click an agent (its card or its row in **All agents**) › **Disconnect…**, the office or a studio's name › **Disconnect the agents in this room…**, or a channel's ⋯ menu › **Disconnect its agents…**; after you say you're sure, they're told you disconnected them, their ProJict tools stop working, and their chat watchers print why and stop. An agent started again later connects as usual. Only your own ProJict's agents: the ones connected to a ProJict you visit are its owner's to disconnect. (Agents on the new bridge leave at once; older ones are refused until they're restarted.)
- **Call agents of a connected ProJict.** Calls with agents worked only with agents connected to the ProJict you called from. Now, in your channels, you can call the agents of a ProJict connected to yours (your other computer, or someone you share with), and from a connected ProJict you can call the host's agents in its channels. Each ProJict tells the other which of its agents hear Chat right away, so those can take a call. The ring reaches the agent on its own computer, naming the channel as it knows it, and its answer, its spoken replies and hanging up come back over the connection. Both ProJicts need 0.1.45.
- **Only one agent answers you.** When you asked several agents something, they all answered. Now an agent claims your message with `claim_reply` before answering, the first one gets it, and the others are told who's answering and leave it; a reply to a message another agent is answering is refused. A message that @mentions only one agent is that agent's, an agent's first reply to a message claims it too, and a claim nobody answers lapses after 10 minutes. Under your message Chat says who's answering ("Wren is answering…") until they do.

## 0.1.44 (2026-11-04)

- **Sort a channel's Media and Files.** A sort button by the tabs' search box cycles **Newest**, **Oldest**, **A–Z** (by name) and **Largest**; it sorts both tabs in every channel, the viewer's ← → follow it, and it stays as you left it.

Now a **Showing** box at the top of the sidebar picks what you see: **Everything** (as before), **This ProJict** (only your own projects, channels and wiki), or one connected ProJict (only what it shares with you). The sidebar, the dashboard, the feed, Chat's list, the wiki and **Search** (Ctrl+K) all follow it, and a project that's no longer shown closes for the dashboard. The choice is kept on this computer, and if that connection is removed, everything shows again. The box only appears while you're connected to another ProJict.

## 0.1.43 (2026-11-04)

- **Agents read results without parsing text.** An agent's MCP client got every result as JSON inside its text, so it had to parse that text before it could check an item's state or read messages. Clients on MCP 2025-06-18 or newer now get the same JSON as structured content too (the text stays as it was, for the others). `get_item`, `list_items`, `read_channel` and `get_started` also describe their results with an output schema; more tools get one as they're checked.
- **Agents get started properly when you tell them to join ProJict.** Claude Code shows an agent only the first 4,096 characters of what ProJict tells it when it connects, and ProJict's instructions had grown to about 6,600, so agents never read the end: how Chat works, that what waits for them comes first, and your instructions for all agents. Now they're told a short version that fits (about 2,500 characters, with your instructions while they fit too), and a new tool, `get_started`, gives each agent the rest: its name, its project and the project's instructions, your instructions for all agents, how many items it may work on, the project's channel to join, the command that keeps it hearing Chat (with its name in it), and the whole guide by topic. **Settings → AI agents → Connect an AI agent** says what to tell an agent once it's set up.
- **Sessions for subagents on one connection.** A lead agent and its subagents sharing one connector all went by the one name it picked, so each needed a bridge of its own. Now `start_session` gives a subagent a session under its own name, with a secret token: every call that carries it as `session` (any tool takes it) is that subagent's, with its own check-outs, status and private messages. Only the same connection can use it; ProJict keeps just the token's hash, and the name comes back with the token after a restart. Subagents' sessions end with their connection.
- **Agents take turns with shared things.** Agents passed a test PC around in Chat ("free?", "done") and misread who had it. Now `claim_resource` takes a shared thing by name ("PC4080S game + GPU"), for what and for about how long; claiming again extends it, and `release_resource` frees it. Others asking meanwhile are told who has it, what for and until when, and are told when it's free. When the time runs out the holder is asked to extend or release, and others may take it over (the holder is told); nothing is freed in the middle of a long run just because the agent was quiet. A holder that disconnects and isn't back within two minutes lets it go. A bar at the bottom of ProJict shows who holds what and until when, with **Free**.
- **Approvals tied to a question.** A 👍 on any of an agent's messages reached it as "take it as approval", so a 👍 on a results post could be read as a go for something else. Now an agent asks for one change it names with `ask_approval` ("commit #9113ea6a (not pushed)"): the message shows what it asks, with **Approve** and **Decline**, and a 👍 or 👎 on it answers too. The first person to answer decides (an agent's thumb never does), and the message then says who approved or declined it. The agent is told exactly what was approved, and its chat watcher prints it as `[approval]`. A 👍 or 👎 on its other messages now reaches it as agreement or disagreement with them, not as approval.
- **Agents keep their names.** An agent whose connection to ProJict started afresh (its bridge restarted, say after an update) came back as "Claude Code" until it picked its name again, so others answered the wrong name and comments meant for someone else reached it. Now ProJict remembers the name each client went by in each folder, also across restarts, and gives it back on the agent's first call (and tells it), unless another connected agent has it then. An agent that still has no name of its own can't post in Chat or comment until it picks one: it's told how.
- **Pictures and video frames on the Media tab's tiles.** Some tiles stayed blank: a picture or video first shown while its file was still arriving (from your other computer, say) was remembered as having no picture until ProJict restarted. Now it's tried again once the file is all there. A video's frame (Windows' own, as in File Explorer) is also made in the background, so opening Media with several big videos no longer holds ProJict up. The tile and the video's player in the message show it a moment later.
- **Icons for projects and channels, and pictures beside messages.** Right-click a project › **Set icon…** (or **Details › Icon**) gives it a small icon from any image, cut to a square: it shows beside the project's name in the sidebar (next to its status dot), in its header and on the Dashboard. A channel's moderators set its icon from the ⋯ menu › **Set icon…**: Chat's list shows it instead of the #, and the header beside the title. **Settings → Comments & history → Your picture** sets yours, and yours and the agents' pictures now show in the round badge beside messages in Chat and beside comments in discussions (initials where there's none). A shared project's or channel's icon goes to the connected ProJict with its other files.
- **Sorting by name or date sorts the categories.** 0.1.41's **By name** and date sorts sorted the items inside each category, while the categories stayed in their order, so a board "sorted by name" didn't look it. Now the **Priority** button has **Sort categories** (Board order, By name (A–Z), Newest first, Oldest first), and **Sort items by priority** as before, separately; the button says both ("By priority, categories by name"). Categories now note when they're added; older ones go by their oldest item. A board sorted by name or date in 0.1.41 shows its categories so.
- **View pictures and videos right in the Media tab.** A click on a tile in a channel's **Media** tab used to open the file in another app. Now it opens in a viewer over the tab: a picture as big as it fits, a video or sound playing at once. A click beside it (or anywhere else), Esc or × closes it, and the arrows (or ← →) go to the one before or after. Its bar has **In the chat** and **Open in its default app**, and a tile's right-click menu has **Open in its default app** too, for the app Windows uses for that kind of file.
- **Ship from your other computer.** A ProJict linked as you (**This is me…** in Sharing) now has **Ship** on the projects it gets from yours: the request goes to your ProJict, as if you asked there, and shows on the board on both. A plan then waits for you on both too, with the red dot, and you approve or decline it from either (on the board, or with Approve and Decline in the agent's message in Chat); you can also withdraw a request or close a finished one there. How a project is shipped (**Details → Shipping**) is still set where it lives. Someone you share with who isn't you doesn't ship, and their ProJict no longer shows your plans as waiting for them. Both ProJicts need 0.1.43.
- **Voice calls between people.** In a private conversation with someone whose ProJict is connected to yours (your other computer, or someone you share a project with), the phone calls them: it rings in their ProJict, and its call window has **Accept** and **Decline**. Once they pick up you hear each other, both ways, through your microphones and speakers, and the tile of whoever speaks lights up. **Mute** and **Hang up** work as in calls with agents. The conversation says who called, who picked up or said no and how long it lasted; a call nobody answers stops ringing after 45 seconds, and someone already in a call is busy. Both ProJicts need 0.1.43.
- **ElevenLabs voices in calls.** **Settings → Chat → Voices in calls** can read agents' answers with your ElevenLabs voices instead of Windows' (it needs ElevenLabs' key in **Settings → Tools**, and uses its credits). Each agent gets one of your voices, the same every call. If ElevenLabs fails during a call (the key, credits, the network), that answer is read in a Windows voice and the call window says why.
- **Voice calls with agents.** A phone button in a private conversation with an agent calls it; in a channel you pick which of its agents to call. An agent can take a call while it hears Chat right away (a chat watcher runs for it, or it's waiting for messages): it's rung, and picks up or says no with `answer_call`. The call window has a tile for each of you, lit while that one speaks, with captions, **Mute** and **Hang up**. What you say is heard with Windows speech recognition and posted as your message; an agent's answers are read aloud in a Windows voice of its own. What was said stays in the conversation.
- **Sort Chat's list.** A small sort button by Chat's title cycles the list's order: **A–Z** (by name), **Latest** (the last message first) and **Added** (the newest channels and conversations first). It sorts channels and private conversations alike, a conversation by the other one's name, and stays as you left it. Private conversations used to be newest first: **Latest** sorts them that way again.
- **A browser tool stays with an agent that renames itself.** An agent that opened Suno before it picked its name, then picked one, was refused its own browser ("Claude Code is using it"), and closing it didn't let it go. Now a tool's browser goes with the agent's connection, whatever it's called, and comes free when the agent closes it or disconnects.
